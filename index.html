ENTREPRENEURIAL JOURNEY
Paycoo

Building a Fintech Startup
From Zero

My entrepreneurial journey with Paycoo — from brand ideation and product development through hackathons, accelerators, and national recognition. Here's how it unfolded.

PHASE 01 — IDEATION & BRAND
Building the Identity
Joined the founding team to shape Paycoo's brand identity and visual direction. Chose purple to represent royalty, trust, and financial richness. The pixelated, gamified logo reflected the loyalty and rewards system at the core of the wallet experience. This phase taught me that branding is not just aesthetics — it's communicating a startup's mission and market positioning.
PHASE 02 — PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT
Building the Ecosystem
Spent nearly one full year building a complete fintech environment: mobile wallet app, POS software for merchants, admin dashboard, cloud infrastructure, KYC verification, offline transactions, loyalty integration, and payment processing. Alongside tech, developed business models, financial forecasts, investor pitch decks, and revenue strategies.
PHASE 03 — MINT HACKATHON
First Major Milestone
Participated in the Mint Hackathon by EG Bank — an intense 3-day business development experience. Refined the startup idea, improved strategy, validated the concept, and presented Paycoo under pressure. Significantly sharpened skills in startup pitching, team collaboration, and problem-solving under deadlines.
PHASE 04 — PROGRAMS & RECOGNITION
Gaining Momentum
Became a finalist at AUC Venture Labs, joined Plug and Play opportunities, and was recognized within fintech and entrepreneurship communities. Accepted into the ITU Seed Program Turkish Incubator Cohort '24. Participated in Seamless North Africa 2024.
PHASE 05 — ACHIEVEMENTS
The Milestones
1st Place — FinTech Track, Hack23 AUC (130 teams, 14 countries) · Top 10 Payment Gateway Startups in Egypt (F6S, 2024) · Featured by Papaya Global · 2nd Place — SU Real Life International Competition 2024 · 2nd Place + Special Award — Rally Egypt Competition, AAST (1,200 teams).

Art, Design & Creative Media
Started at 14 · Former Applied Arts student
🎨

Art and design are part of my lifestyle and personality. After high school, I joined the Faculty of Applied Arts for a year before transferring to Computer Science — to combine creativity with technology. My journey started at 14 when I discovered Photoshop and was amazed that something I designed on a screen could become a real printed product or campaign.

One memory that stayed with me: restoring an old scratched photo of my father and seeing how happy it made him. That's when I realized the emotional impact design can have. I later contributed as a graphic designer in TEDx and IEEE during high school, then moved into professional freelancing and now work as a Multimedia & UI/UX Designer.
