RESEARCH
Publication

Quantum-Safe Connected Vehicles

Vehicles broadcast safety messages ten times a second. Post-quantum signatures are big. Put the two together naively and the radio channel collapses. This paper is about not doing that naively.

2026 INTERNATIONAL CONFERENCE ON ADVANCEMENTS IN SMART, SECURE AND INTELLIGENT COMPUTING (ASSIC)
BHUBANESWAR, INDIA · PP. 1–6 · IEEE
A Post-Quantum Aware SCMS for V2X Communications Using Selective Cryptographic Methodology
A. Fikry, E. R. Diab, M. Mohamed, M. M. Atef, K. K. Elfiky, A. S. Mohamed, S. A. ElSagheer

Quantum computers will break the elliptic-curve signatures that today's V2X Security Credential Management Systems depend on. Replacing them wholesale with post-quantum schemes is not an option either: with full PQC, DSRC channel utilization reached 161%, far beyond capacity. We propose a selective methodology inside the IEEE 1609.2 framework: CRYSTALS-Dilithium-2 and Kyber-768 (ML-KEM) protect the credential lifecycle, where a quantum adversary has years to attack, while ECDSA stays on the high-frequency CAM/DENM safety messages. Channel utilization drops to 4.3% with the long-lived trust chain made quantum-safe.
